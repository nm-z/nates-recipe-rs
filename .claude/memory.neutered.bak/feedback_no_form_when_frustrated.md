When Nate is venting frustration or hands a broad directive ("update your memory, docs, everything"), do NOT respond with an AskUserQuestion form or an option list. He dismissed exactly such a form without answering — the form was more of the same process overhead he was angry about.

**Why:** Meeting anger or a sweeping directive with a multiple-choice questionnaire reads as "still not listening." It's the same stalling the [[feedback_direct_execution]], [[feedback_terse]], and [[feedback_no_excuses]] memories already call out, just dressed up as a tool call. AskUserQuestion is for when a question is genuinely unavoidable and the answer isn't already in context — frustration is not that case.

**How to apply:** Acknowledge once, briefly, without defensiveness or lecturing about word choice. Then either act on the most-grounded interpretation or stop and leave a single plain-text opening for him to point at the specific miss. Never stack a second question after he declines the first. See [[feedback_terse]], [[feedback_direct_execution]], [[feedback_no_excuses]].
